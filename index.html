What we do

A collaborative lab for curious minds who want to ship ideas into papers.

We are a student-led research group at CMU. Members pick ambitious ML questions, run disciplined experiments, read the literature together, and push toward submissions at elite venues.

Research agenda

Topics we explore

Interests evolve with the field. Right now we orbit questions around how models reason, how they fail, and how to make them trustworthy and useful at scale.

  • LLMs & reasoningChain-of-thought, faithfulness, agents
  • InterpretabilityMechanistic insight into trained systems
  • RL & decision-makingPolicy learning, reward design, control
  • Vision & multimodalityGrounded perception and generation
  • EfficiencyCompression, distillation, scalable training
  • Safety & alignmentRobustness, evaluation, responsible deployment
